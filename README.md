## What This Demonstrates

This case pack shows how I approach a messy cross-functional process before building any automation. It covers current-state discovery, pain point analysis, future-state design, requirements translation, risk and dependency tracking, and KPI framing.

This is the core work of an AI Process Innovation role: turning ambiguous business pain into structured, implementation-ready artifacts.

## Problem

Employee offboarding is one of the most error-prone cross-functional processes in any organization. When it breaks, access is not revoked on time, equipment is not returned, HR records are not closed, and the employee experience suffers. This case pack redesigns the process to be traceable, automatable, and measurable.
