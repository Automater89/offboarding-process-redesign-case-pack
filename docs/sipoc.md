# SIPOC: Employee Offboarding Process

A SIPOC maps the Suppliers, Inputs, Process steps, Outputs, and Customers of a process at a high level. It is used here to define process scope before mapping detailed flows.

---

## SIPOC Table

| Supplier | Input | Process Step | Output | Customer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HR Business Partner | Resignation letter or termination decision | 1. Confirm departure and set last day | Confirmed offboarding record | IT, Security, Payroll, Manager |
| Manager | Last day confirmation, role context | 2. Notify all stakeholders with tasks and deadlines | Automated task assignments | IT, Security, Payroll, HR |
| IT | Access inventory, device list | 3. Revoke system access and accounts | Confirmed access revocation | HR, Security |
| Security | Badge and physical access records | 4. Deactivate badge and building access | Confirmed physical access closure | HR |
| Manager / Employee | Equipment list, project files | 5. Coordinate knowledge transfer and equipment return | Completed checklist, returned equipment | Manager, IT, HR |
| Payroll | Final timesheet, PTO balance | 6. Process final pay and benefits close | Final paycheck, benefits termination | Employee |
| HR Business Partner | Exit interview notes, documentation | 7. Close HR record and archive | Closed employee record | HR, Legal |

---

## Scope Notes

- **Start:** Resignation received or termination decision confirmed
- **End:** HR record closed, all access revoked, equipment returned, final pay issued
- **Out of scope:** Recruitment backfill, alumni communications, reference checks
